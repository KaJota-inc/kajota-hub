# Lifecycle Workflows — the application campaign, run the MELSAN way

*These fire on specific days (see `03-cadence-and-gates.md`), not every day. Each drives a cluster of
workbook tasks across the Professor Pipeline, Supervisor Tracker, Risk Register, Verified Sources and
Weekly Scorecard sheets. Same temperament split: **Melancholic verifies and builds the evidence;
Sanguine does the human contact and keeps morale.***

---

## A · Professor-First Outreach *(Weeks 1–5, per lead)*

The workbook's north rule: **treat fit as a hypothesis until the professor confirms it**, and
**fit ≠ eligibility ≠ funding — three separate checks.** Eight leads are shortlisted (Demetriou,
Casale, Song Wang, Toni, Fokaefs, Nguyen, Fan, Belardinelli).

Per lead, run the stages (Shortlisted → Research reviewed → Ready to contact → Contacted → Replied →
Meeting → Funding/route clarified):

1. **[MEL] Review the evidence.** Read the official profile + 2–3 recent papers/the QRT project
   description. Produce a **5-bullet fit map**: what matches our thesis, what does not. Cite sources.
   No outreach before this note exists.
2. **[MEL] Verify the three checks.** (a) Research fit — the map above. (b) Vacancy/scope — is the
   project open, is the supervisor taking students? (c) Eligibility — does our route survive the
   Imperial Bachelor's-rule / York direct-entry question? Mark each unknown explicitly in the
   Supervisor Tracker; an unknown is not a green light.
3. **[SAN] Compose the approach — in your own words.** One concrete connection to a specific
   paper/project + one short, genuine fit question. Warm, specific, never a generic copy-paste pitch
   (the workbook bans duplicate generic pitches, especially across the coordinated SD-1/SD-2
   Demetriou–Toni context). **You write it; this pack never drafts it for you.**
4. **[MEL] Pre-send scrutiny.** Contact detail verified on the official profile (not assumed).
   Names, project titles, and the prescribed QRT title format correct. No claim in the email that
   isn't true today.
5. **[SAN] Send + [MEL] log.** Record in Professor Pipeline: stage, last-action date, next action,
   follow-up date, open questions. Silence is data — set the follow-up date now.

**Priority order (from the workbook):** 1) Demetriou (QRT SD-2, machine-checkable obligations in
financial pipelines) · 2) Song Wang (York, agent/AI reliability — also carries the direct-entry
eligibility question) · 3) Casale (adaptive inference as resource allocation). Toni via SD-1, Fan via
HF-1, Belardinelli via FB-1 as project-routed; Nguyen/secondary — do **not** let the Web3 angle widen
the thesis into generic blockchain security.

## B · Sunday Exit-Gate Review *(every Sunday)*

The Weekly Scorecard rule: **assess outputs and set next week's correction — not hours studied.**

1. **[MEL] Score the week against the exit gate.** Each week has a specific gate (e.g. W1 "baseline
   saved; eligibility/funding risks explicitly logged"; W3 "another engineer could understand how
   the research would be tested"; W6 "no critical unsupported claims or missing documents remain").
   Pass / partial / fail with evidence.
2. **[MEL] Mine the error logs.** Pull the week's highest-error IELTS criterion and DSA pattern.
   Those become next week's targeted repair — not new material.
3. **[SAN] Credit + reframe.** Name three wins aloud; name the single most important correction for
   next week in one encouraging sentence. Protect the 49-day stamina deliberately.
4. **[MEL+SAN] Set the week.** Write next week's one measurable correction per domain into the
   Weekly Scorecard; confirm which lifecycle workflows fire (`03`).

## C · Compliance & Verification Gate *(before any submission, and on every statement/claim day)*

This workflow exists to stop two failures that a strong proposal cannot undo.

1. **[MEL] The no-GenAI statement check.** Confirm the personal (1–2 pp) and research (3–4 pp)
   statements are **authored by you**, prose and all. This pack and the workbook may hold the
   skeleton, the requirement checklist, and the adversarial critique — never generated sentences.
   Re-read Imperial's current guidance before submitting in case the rule's wording changed.
2. **[MEL] The claim-by-claim fact check.** Every load-bearing claim in CV/statements/emails →
   traced to the Verified Sources sheet or re-verified on the official page. Remove anything you
   cannot source. Confidential Remita/Ecobank data and production credentials never appear; the
   testbed is synthetic/public only.
3. **[MEL] The eligibility reality check.** Imperial Bachelor's-only risk → is the post-degree
   experience evidence assembled and is the December round realistic? York Chemical-Eng direct-entry
   → status recorded as *confirmed / not eligible / unresolved*; if unresolved, the email is sent and
   the MSc/MASc fallback is documented. **Do not submit on an assumption.**
4. **[MEL] Document & QA.** Cross-check names, project references, QRT title format, file versions,
   dates, uploads, ≥2 (Imperial) / 3 (York) references actually received by the institution.
5. **[SAN] Submission call.** Submit early when complete and route-appropriate; save the receipt and
   the exact submitted files. If blocked, log the precise requirement and the next viable round — a
   documented hold beats a knowingly incomplete submission.

## D · Risk-Register Sweep *(start of each week + before submission)*

1. **[MEL] Walk the ten risks.** For each (Imperial entry, York subject fit, GenAI-statement rule,
   QRT slot/fit, funding-vs-guarantee, York funding amount, novelty breadth, IELTS target-vs-minimum,
   deadline/timezone): update likelihood, impact, status.
2. **[MEL] Assign owner + dated next action** to every Open/High risk. A risk without a dated action
   is not being managed.
3. **[SAN] Decide and move.** For each red risk, make the go / hold / reroute call out loud and
   record it. Decisiveness is the Sanguine antidote to Melancholic over-caution — but only *after*
   the Melancholic verification, never instead of it.

## The lifecycle creed

*Fit is a hypothesis; verify it. Eligibility is a gate; don't assume past it. The statement is yours;
write every word. Submit early when true, hold loudly when not.*
