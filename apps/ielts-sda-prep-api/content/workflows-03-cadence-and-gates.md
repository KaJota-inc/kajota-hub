## The rhythm at a glance

- **Every day:** **MELSAN-Launch** (morning ~20m) → the four domain blocks, each a **MELSAN-Execute**
  run → **MELSAN-Close** (evening ~25m). Artefacts to `day-packs/<date>/`, mirrored to the workbook.
- **Every Sunday:** Exit-Gate Review (`02-B`).
- **Every Monday (week start):** Risk-Register Sweep (`02-D`).
- **Outreach (`02-A`):** rolling through Weeks 1–5, in priority order.
- **Compliance & Verification Gate (`02-C`):** on every statement/claim day, and hard before any submit.

## Gate discipline (the Melancholic spine of the whole clock)

A week does not "end" on Sunday by calendar — it ends when its **exit gate passes with evidence**. If
a gate fails, the Sunday review (`02-B`) carries the miss into next week as the priority correction;
it is not silently dropped. The Sanguine keeps morale through the carry-over; the Melancholic refuses
to mark a gate green without proof. That tension, held for seven weeks, is the campaign.
