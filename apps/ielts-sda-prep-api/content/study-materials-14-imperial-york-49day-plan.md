## 🚨 Compliance box — read first, re-read on each statement day

**Imperial official guidance:** the personal statement (1–2 pages) and the research statement (3–4 pages) *must be written by the applicant without GenAI help*. This file, and anything I generate, is **planning scaffolding only** — requirement checklists, source lists, outline structures, methodology prompts, mock-defence questions. Imperial statement text must come from your own keystrokes.

What I **can** do:
- Produce System Design diagrams and 1-pagers for daily study.
- Produce DSA solutions in Java 21 with tests and complexity notes.
- Produce PhD research artefacts: literature matrices, methodology prompts, metrics specifications, failure-mode catalogues, baseline specifications.
- Produce IELTS Reading passages, Listening scripts, Writing-criteria scoring sheets, Speaking cue-card decks, cue-card follow-ups.
- Pre-stage research-fit notes for supervisors.
- Pre-stage mock-defence question banks and research-interview rubrics.
- Track every submission requirement against official sources and flag drift.
- Audit the final application for factual accuracy, formatting and source citation.

What I **cannot** do:
- Draft, co-write, "polish", or ghostwrite the Imperial personal or research statement.
- Rewrite your own statement text in any way that changes voice or substance.
- Supply "sample paragraphs" that could end up in a submission.

The York statement (one-pager) has no equivalent public rule in this workbook; even so, treat it the same for simplicity: your writing, with my scaffolding.

## Verified facts (checked 14 Oct 2026)

- **Imperial QRT 2027 intake** · rounds 1 Oct · 1 Dec · 1 Feb · 1 Apr. Dec round = target. · [source](https://qrt-labs.imperial.ac.uk/admissions)
- **QRT funding** · 3.5 years, tuition + standard Imperial stipend, Home + Overseas eligible. Competitive; not guaranteed.
- **QRT application** · Imperial Computing PhD admissions portal; no separate scholarship application; up to 3 project references *or* own topic; title must include references in prescribed format.
- **Imperial academic entry** · First-Class/Distinction Master's expected; Bachelor's-only not normally considered; outstanding candidates with extensive relevant post-degree experience may still apply; exceptions not evaluated by email.
- **Imperial statements** · CV · 1–2 page personal statement · 3–4 page research statement · transcripts · ≥ 2 references. **Statements must be written by the applicant without GenAI help.**
- **York Fall application window** · 1 Oct – 1 Dec. · [source](https://lassonde.yorku.ca/eecs/academics/graduate/future-students/)
- **York PhD normal route** · Master's in computer engineering, CS, EE, SE or closely related; minimum B+ course average.
- **York direct entry** · Bachelor's in named computing/engineering OR *closely related field* + research experience + A average. **Chemical Engineering fit is unresolved and must not be assumed.**
- **York IELTS minimum** · 7.0 (per EECS calendar). *Target 8.0 / 7.5 each is personal.*
- **IELTS Academic Writing** · Task 1 ~ 20 min ≥ 150 words; Task 2 ~ 40 min ≥ 250 words; Task 2 weighted 2× Task 1. **Speaking** · 11–14 min, four criteria (FC / LR / GRA / P).
