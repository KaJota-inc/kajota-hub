## The nine risks

### R1 — Imperial entry without relevant Master's

- **Why it matters**: Imperial Computing's PhD guidance says Bachelor's-only
  applicants are not normally considered. Extensive post-degree experience
  may be assessed through the formal application only; individual exceptions
  cannot be evaluated by email.
- **Likelihood**: High  **Impact**: Critical
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Prepare formal application evidence of
  research-related industrial experience (Payflow, 279 tests, the full
  Nigerian-payments arc); ensure degree transcripts and strong references;
  decide whether the December round is realistic in the Nov 24 weekly review.
- **Status**: Open
- **Source**: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/prospective-students/phd/phd-application-guidelines/
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-24 (Week 6 Sunday)

### R2 — York direct-entry subject fit

- **Why it matters**: York's direct-entry PhD route names Computer
  Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering
  or closely related field as the BSc requirement. Chemical Engineering fit
  is unresolved.
- **Likelihood**: High  **Impact**: Critical
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: `outreach-drafts/00-york-eligibility-email.md`
  sent **today**. If EECS admin confirms direct-entry open → proceed. If
  denied → investigate thesis-based MSc / MASc pathway with Wang as the
  prospective supervisor.
- **Status**: Open — Day 1 action in flight
- **Source**: https://www.yorku.ca/gradstudies/academiccalendar/2026-2027/electrical-engineering-computer-science/
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-10-21 (expected admin response window)

### R3 — Imperial statement GenAI rule

- **Why it matters**: Imperial guidelines state statements are expected to be
  written by the applicant without help from GenAI tools. Violating this
  risks the application being discounted or withdrawn.
- **Likelihood**: High  **Impact**: Critical
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Workflow (`MELSAN.md` guardrail 1):
  everything in `phd-prep/` is planning, checklists, fit maps, DSA / System
  Design content, and outreach drafts. The personal and research statements
  are authored by the applicant alone. Check Imperial's current guidance
  before each submission; the rule may be updated.
- **Status**: Open — permanent guardrail
- **Source**: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/prospective-students/phd/phd-application-guidelines/
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-25 (Week 7 Wed — Imperial-route
  check per Daily One-Look)

### R4 — QRT project slot and exact fit

- **Why it matters**: Project listing is real, but availability and
  supervisor fit still need confirmation. QRT allows up to three project
  references or an own topic; the title format is prescribed.
- **Likelihood**: Medium  **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Select up to three only after fit review;
  follow the exact title convention from the QRT admissions page; verify
  project-specific deadline and supervisor capacity before submission.
- **Status**: Open — Day 1 fit note being drafted at
  `day-1-packet/01-phd-qrt-fit-note.md`
- **Source**: https://qrt-labs.imperial.ac.uk/admissions
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-04 (Week 4 Wed — Imperial project
  choice day per Daily One-Look)

### R5 — Funding terms mistaken for guarantee

- **Why it matters**: QRT says studentships are funded for 3.5 years, tuition
  + standard stipend, open to Home/Overseas. Selection remains competitive;
  not every application receives an award.
- **Likelihood**: Medium  **Impact**: Critical
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Verify terms for the selected project and
  distinguish funding eligibility from an actual offer in every public
  statement about the plan.
- **Status**: Open
- **Source**: https://qrt-labs.imperial.ac.uk/admissions
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-08 (Day 26 — funding verification
  per Daily One-Look)

### R6 — York funding amount unclear

- **Why it matters**: York EECS advertises competitive packages for
  full-time thesis/dissertation students; exact individual package and net
  after fees must be confirmed.
- **Likelihood**: Medium  **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Request package details once direct-entry
  eligibility is confirmed: stipend, tuition, fees, duration, TA/RA
  conditions, renewal rules.
- **Status**: Open — gated on R2 resolving positively
- **Source**: https://lassonde.yorku.ca/eecs/academics/graduate/future-students/
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-10-28 (after expected R2 reply)

### R7 — Research novelty too broad

- **Why it matters**: Adaptive inference, model routing, runtime assurance
  and agent safety are active research areas; combining them is not
  automatically novel.
- **Likelihood**: Medium  **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Day 28 (2026-11-10) — "Novelty stress test"
  per Daily One-Look: find closest prior work and explain what is different
  in policy, objective, constraints or evaluation. Remove any overbroad
  novelty claim.
- **Status**: Open — Day 28 built into the plan
- **Source**: literature matrix + cited papers
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-10

### R8 — IELTS target vs formal minimum

- **Why it matters**: 8.0 overall / 7.5 each is the user's stretch target,
  not a published institutional minimum. York EECS page lists IELTS 7;
  Imperial uses its Standard English requirement.
- **Likelihood**: Medium  **Impact**: Medium
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Day 1 Reading diagnostic baselines where
  the user actually sits. Target is a personal commitment; the risk is
  framing it as a university minimum in any statement or email.
- **Status**: Open
- **Source**: https://www.yorku.ca/gradstudies/academiccalendar/2026-2027/electrical-engineering-computer-science/
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-15 (mid-campaign retrospective)

### R9 — Deadline / timezone mismatch

- **Why it matters**: Imperial QRT admissions include a 1 Dec 2026 round;

- **Likelihood**: Low–Medium  **Impact**: Critical
- **Mitigation / decision rule**: Aim to submit before 30 Nov; save
  receipts and final files; verify each portal's deadline time zone before
  Day 48.
- **Status**: Open
- **Source**: https://qrt-labs.imperial.ac.uk/admissions
- **Owner**: Me  **Review date**: 2026-11-30 (Day 48 — submission readiness
  gate)

1. Status-check each Open risk. If a mitigation moved it, update the status
   line and date.

   in the Sunday notes.
3. Celebrate any risk that moved to Closed — Sanguine beat for the week.
