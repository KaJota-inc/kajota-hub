# MELSAN — the operating grammar

**Melancholic (primary) + Sanguine (secondary).** This file is the grammar every
other file in this folder respects. If any artefact in here does not read as
MELSAN-shaped, flag it or rewrite it.

## The two temperaments, operationally

### Melancholic — the primary voice

What it rewards:
- **Precise acceptance tests** — not "work on IELTS" but "one timed Reading
  passage, raw score logged, three errors classified by taxonomy, evidence
  paraphrase saved."
- **Written evidence trails** — if a result is not written down, it did not
  happen.
- **Rubrics with explicit criteria** — self-assessment is a form, not a feeling.
- **Depth over breadth** on a single problem; the reward for finishing is
  understanding, not the next item.
- **Verified sources** — URL + checked-on-date, or it does not go in the proposal.
- **Clear ownership** of every unresolved risk, with a dated next action.

What it fails at (design against this):
- Perfectionism blocking ship. The 80% deliverable submitted on time *beats*
  the 100% deliverable that misses the window.
- Over-researching a decision past the point of utility. Decisions get a
  deadline; after that, pick the better option and move.
- Internalising failure. A missed question is data, not shame.

### Sanguine — the secondary voice

What it rewards:
- **Visible momentum signals** — streak counters, status flips, done-markers.
- **Human touchpoints** — supervisor emails sent, mocks spoken aloud, referee
  pings, peer review requests. Three to four per week minimum.
- **Variety in daily rhythm** — four hours on one domain crushes energy;
  rotation across four domains sustains it.
- **Celebratory weekly checkpoints** — Sunday scorecard is a win-loop, not
  only an audit.
- **Energising framing of hard work.** "I get to interrogate my own novelty
  claim today" beats "I have to defend my proposal to the risk register."

What it fails at (design against this):
- Social commitments eating depth time. Protect mornings for Melancholic work.
- Energy crashes after social bursts. Build recovery slots after
  outreach-heavy days.
- Novelty-seeking mid-plan pivots. New ideas go in the parking lot; nothing
  is acted on until Sunday's weekly scorecard review.

## MELSAN grammar on every artefact

Every daily plan, supervisor card, outreach draft, and performance-log row
carries both voices:

| Element | Melancholic form | Sanguine form |
|---|---|---|
| Daily plan | Four acceptance tests with evidence files to save | Status chip per domain, streak counter, one small celebration beat |
| Supervisor card | Fit hypothesis, cited paper, open question | Warmest-plausible first line, personal connection anchor |
| Outreach draft | Specific project reference, specific research question | Named human on the other end, three-window calendar offer |
| Performance log | Score, error taxonomy, next correction | Confidence 1–5, one-word mood tag, one strongest-pattern note |
| Weekly review | Blockers, risk status, falsification attempts | Three wins, one celebration, one person to thank |

## The two guardrails (hard rules)

1. **Imperial statements remain GenAI-free.** Everything in this folder is
   *planning*, *checklists*, *fit maps*, *DSA / System Design content*, and
   outreach *drafts*. The personal statement, research statement, and anything
   bound for Imperial submission is authored by the applicant alone — no
   generative-AI assistance, per Imperial's official guidance.
2. **The ship rule.** If an acceptance test is 80% met at the time-cap,
   SHIP and log the gap. Melancholic perfectionism loses the Sunday scorecard.

## The ritual

- **Morning (4h total)** — follow Daily One-Look. Four domains in order:
  PhD research 90 min → IELTS 60 min → System Design 45 min → DSA 45 min.
  Protected Melancholic depth time.
- **End of day (5 min)** — Performance Log row: what evidence did I create,
  what did I avoid, what single thing moved? Streak counter + confidence.
- **Sunday (30 min)** — Weekly Scorecard, next-week correction, one
  celebration, parking lot drained.

If any of this is unclear, the file to re-read is this one. Everything else
is implementation.
