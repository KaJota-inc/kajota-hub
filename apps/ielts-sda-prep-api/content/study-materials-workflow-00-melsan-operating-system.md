# MELSAN Operating System — the Melancholic-Sanguine way to run the 49-day campaign

*Companion to `~/Downloads/Imperial_York_Premium_PhD_Prep_Plan.xlsx`. The workbook holds the
**what** (49 daily plans, four domains, acceptance tests, gates). This pack holds the **how** — a
personality-driven execution system so the document's tasks are not merely attempted but vividly
accomplished.*

---

## Who is running this campaign

You operate as a **Melancholic-Sanguine** — the rare blend that makes an outstanding researcher
*and* a convincing one.

- **Melancholic (your primary, the builder).** Analytical, systematic, exacting. It loves the deep
  single-threaded work: the literature matrix, the falsifiable hypothesis, the invariant proof, the
  complexity analysis, the fact-check against the official page. It is where *correctness and depth*
  come from. Its shadow is perfectionism, over-caution, and discouragement when the work is hard.
- **Sanguine (your secondary, the communicator).** Warm, expressive, optimistic, fluent with
  people. It is where the *tailored professor email, the two-minute spoken answer, the viva presence,
  and the stamina to show up for 49 days* come from. Its shadow is over-promising, scatter, and
  gliding past detail.

The campaign works because the two halves **divide the labour and police each other**:

| The work needs… | Led by | The other half's job |
|---|---|---|
| Research depth, verification, acceptance tests | **Melancholic** | Sanguine keeps it moving — "ship the note, don't polish forever" |
| Outreach, speaking, viva, morale | **Sanguine** | Melancholic keeps it honest — "every claim verified, nothing generic" |

> Rule of the blend: **Melancholic builds it true; Sanguine makes it land.** Neither ships alone.

## The MELSAN daily loop (run it on every task, all four domains)

Six moves. Each is tagged with the temperament that leads it, so you consciously switch gears.

1. **[MEL] Measure the target.** Name the *smallest meaningful output* and its acceptance test from
   the workbook, before touching anything. "Done" is the acceptance test passing — never time spent.
2. **[SAN] Engage warm.** Start out loud: say in one sentence what you're about to produce and why it
   matters to the thesis. This primes energy and doubles as speaking practice.
3. **[MEL] Labour deeply.** Single-threaded, no tabs you don't need. Produce the artefact — the
   diagram, the proof, the tested code, the fit-note, the corrected essay.
4. **[MEL] Scrutinise & source-verify.** Attack your own output. Is the claim verified against an
   official page? Does the test cover the edge case? Is the novelty claim defensible? Log the errors.
5. **[SAN] Articulate aloud.** Explain the finished artefact in two minutes, plainly, as if to a
   supervisor. If you can't say it simply, the Melancholic work isn't finished. This *is* the
   IELTS-speaking and viva rep, taken for free from real content.
6. **[MEL+SAN] Note the next action + save evidence.** Record one dated next step and file the proof
   in the workbook (Performance Log / Daily One-Look). Leave the campsite ready for tomorrow.

The loop is the personality in motion: Melancholic owns moves 1, 3, 4; Sanguine owns 2, 5; both own 6.

## The three named workflows (your canonical MELSAN — reference them by name)

The daily loop above lives *inside* the middle workflow. Your established MELSAN defines three
named routines that bracket the day; use these names when you run them.

- **MELSAN-Launch (morning, ~20 min).** 5 min — read today's four acceptance tests aloud, flag any
  ambiguity *[MEL]*. 10 min — stage the exact source links, prior error log, and verification targets
  you'll need *[MEL]*. 5 min — name, in writing, the one **flagship win** that would make today a
  victory *[SAN]*.
- **MELSAN-Execute (per block, 45/60/90 min).** Run the six-move loop above, once per domain block.
  Zero multitasking; 25/5 Pomodoro cadence inside; a 60-second halfway self-check — *"am I hitting the
  acceptance test or drifting?"* *[MEL]*. Save the artefact immediately, no post-hoc write-up *[MEL]*.
  One-sentence ship log when the block ends *[SAN]*.
- **MELSAN-Close (evening, ~25 min).** 10 min *[SAN]* — name what shipped today, one line each.
  10 min *[MEL]* — honest error log: what missed, what to repeat. 5 min — queue tomorrow's four
  acceptance tests + tomorrow's one flagship win.

**Rigour first, warmth second — always in that order.** Melancholic correctness is the floor;
Sanguine momentum is built on top of it, never in place of it.

**Save convention.** Every artefact lands in `day-packs/<date>/` under the campaign root
(`~/Documents/imperial-york-phd/day-packs/2026-10-14/`, etc.), *and* its status/evidence is mirrored
into the workbook's Performance Log and Daily One-Look. The day-pack is the working copy; the
workbook is the system of record.

## Two gates that override everything (from the workbook's own rules)

These are not preferences; the workbook and the official sources state them. The MELSAN system
treats breaching them as a failed day regardless of output.

1. **No-GenAI statement gate.** Imperial's guidance expects the personal statement (1–2 pp) and
   research statement (3–4 pp) to be **written by you, without generative-AI help.** Every workflow
   here that touches a statement does scaffolding, checklisting, and adversarial critique **only** —
   it never drafts the prose. The Sanguine voice you build in the daily *Articulate* move is what
   fills the page, in your own words. This is a feature: the viva will test whether the voice is
   yours.
2. **Verify-before-assume gate.** Eligibility and fit are hypotheses until an official page or the
   institution confirms them. Two live red risks: *Imperial does not normally consider Bachelor's-only
   candidates* (your route depends on the post-degree-experience exception, assessed formally), and
   *York's direct-PhD route names computing/engineering degrees — Chemical Engineering fit is
   unresolved.* The Melancholic *Scrutinise* move checks every load-bearing claim against the
   Verified Sources sheet before it enters an application.
